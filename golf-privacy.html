Golf Scorecard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Information we collect

Golf Scorecard requires no sign-up or login and does not collect personally identifying information. Course names, player names and scores you enter are stored only on your device and are never sent to a server.

Advertising

The app shows a banner ad via Google AdMob, which may collect an advertising identifier and device information to serve ads. See the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob data disclosure.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
